Three steps, none of them yours

1

Drop in your material

A PDF, slides or messy notes. The file never leaves your machine.

2

Let the model read it

Gemma, an open-weight model, splits the text into sections and writes the kit one section at a time.

3

Revise in any order

Flip the cards, take the quiz, or listen to the brief on the way to the exam.

Why it runs on your machine

It works offline

Exam season and bad Wi-Fi go together. The model, the voice and the database are all local.

Your notes stay yours

No server and no one else reading your lecture notes or your wrong answers.

You can change the model

Point one setting at a different open-weight model and everything else keeps working.
